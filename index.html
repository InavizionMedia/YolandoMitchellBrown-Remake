About

Short bio — copy/paste for programs sample

Yolando Mitchell Brown is a Virtual Creative Entrepreneur — coach, business owner, and producer of the Every Way Woman talk show. She helps entrepreneurs and aspiring hosts show up with confidence on camera, on stage, and online.

Hi, I'm Yolando Mitchell Brown — Student of Life

I'm from East Saint Louis, Illinois. In 2000 I went back to school — LA Valley College while waiting tables in Beverly Hills, an ASU member and Commissioner of Environmental Affairs. I transferred to Cal State Dominguez Hills on a Presidential Scholarship, and there I began taping my talk show, Every Way Woman, from home. I went on to earn my MA at Cal Poly Pomona.

A studio staffer once told me, "this is not your studio." So I built my own: Inavizion Media opened in 2015, and My Creative Space followed three years later.
